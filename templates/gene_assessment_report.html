Each gene lists its gene-disease-MoI associations. Ratings after an arrow are the evidence in this corpus: they are computed from the papers this run read for the gene (new papers, tournament picks and publications PanelApp already cites), not from everything published. They are not recommendations; a corpus rating below PanelApp's rating is not a downgrade recommendation. A gene's header shows its top association's rating.
