KEY INSIGHT - DISEASE ENTITIES AND INHERITANCE:
A gene may cause multiple DISTINCT diseases (disease entities), and the SAME phenotype may show different modes of inheritance. Each combination needs independent evidence. For example:
- Phenotype A with biallelic inheritance: Epilepsy syndrome (5 unrelated families)

These are counted SEPARATELY, not summed. Each phenotype-inheritance combination needs ~3+ unrelated families.

   - Recent comprehensive reviews or large cohort studies (PREFERRED for information density)

   - For well-established phenotypes: ONE comprehensive recent paper is better than multiple older partial papers

3. EVIDENCE STRENGTH - Sufficient pathogenicity evidence per disease entity
   - Multiple truly UNRELATED families (not same founder variant, not re-analysis of same cohorts)

   - Note: ~3-5 unrelated families usually meets the bar; 20 ≈ 200 for our purposes

4. RECENCY - When phenotype coverage is similar, strongly prefer recent papers
